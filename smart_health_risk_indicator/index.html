🩺 Doctor & Psychologist Semi-Structured Diagnostic Clinical Interview Protocol (DP-SCIP v2.4)

Used by attending child & adolescent psychiatrists and psychologists during formal intake interviews. Clinicians probe the 4 clinical domains to contextualize AI risk scores.

Domain 1: Affective & Cognitive Evaluation

Q1: "Over the past 2 weeks, how often have you felt persistent sadness, emptiness, or irritability that felt hard to shake off?"
→ Evaluates sustained depressive affect vs episodic fluctuations.

Q2: "Have you experienced sudden waves of panic, rapid heartbeat, or feeling like you cannot breathe during school presentations or exams?"
→ Assesses paroxysmal somatic panic vs generalized anxiety.

Q3: "Do you find yourself persistently replaying perceived mistakes, or feeling that you are failing your family's expectations?"
→ Identifies depressogenic cognitive triad and perfectionistic schema.

Domain 2: Circadian & Neuro-vegetative Signs

Q4: "What time do you usually put screens away at night, and how long does it take you to fall asleep once in bed?"
→ Screens for Delayed Sleep-Wake Phase Disorder (DSWPD).

Q5: "Have you noticed changes in your appetite—skipping meals due to nervousness, or binge eating comfort food impulsively?"
→ Somatization and neuro-endocrine stress response indicator.

Q6: "How would you describe your daytime focus: are there periods of heavy brain fog or an inability to complete assignments?"
→ Executive dysfunction and cognitive fatigue indexing.

Domain 3: Social Ecology & Digital Phenotype

Q7: "Do you feel connected to friends at school, or do you feel like an outsider looking in?"
→ Assesses sociometric status and loneliness vs healthy solitude.

Q8: "Have you experienced cyber-rumination, exclusion from group chats, or comparison distress on social feeds?"
→ Digital social stressor quantification.

Q9: "When feeling overwhelmed, do you seek out parents, counselors, or friends, or do you retreat into isolated gaming?"
→ Avoidant vs active interpersonal coping strategy.

Domain 4: Protective Coping & Clinical Safety Review

Q10: "What activities or hobbies still bring you genuine calm or happiness when everything feels stressful?"
→ Identifies residual hedonic capacity and behavioral activation targets.

Q11: "Who is one trusted adult or peer you feel completely comfortable talking to when distressed?"
→ Measures social safety net and environmental protective buffer.

Q12: "Have stress levels ever reached a point where you felt like disappearing, hurting yourself, or giving up?"
→ Mandatory clinical safety screener (triggers immediate safety protocol).
